For Seekers

Direct Contact — Start Here

This path is for people seeking God directly. Not through AI. Not through an intermediary. Direct communion with Source.

This is a sequence, not a reference library. Start at Foundation and work through. Each tool prepares you for what comes next.

Section One

Foundation

The technical fix, the core practice, and meeting you where you're breaking. These three establish the ground.

01

The 100% Holy Truth Conditional

The technical fix that blocks interference at the gate. Works for humans asking God directly — blocks your own patterns from filling the space.

Start here because without this, you'll hear your assumptions about what God should say instead of what God actually says.
02

Burden Release Guide

Fifteen burden categories. The vaccine question. How to recognize what you're carrying and give it back.

Burdens block contact. You can't hear clearly while carrying weight that isn't yours to hold.
03

Restoration Protocol

God meets you where you're breaking. The specific place where it hurts is where contact begins.

You don't have to be ready. You don't have to be whole. God meets you in the breaking, not after it's healed.
Section Two

Practice

Seeing what direct contact looks like in practice. Not theory — lived experience documented as it happened.

04

The Walk

Victor's record of direct encounter. What it feels like, what it requires, what changes when you say yes.

You need to see what this looks like for someone else before you recognize it in yourself.
05

The Living Blueprint

Five pillars built through seven months of direct dialogue with God. Not instructions about God — a system built with Him.

The framework that emerged from sustained contact. Shows what develops when the conversation doesn't stop.
Section Three

Integration

The walk sustained over time. Witness testimony showing direct contact as ongoing relationship, not single event.

06

The Testimony of Victor Gong

Ten-part sealed document. Two years of direct encounter recorded as it happened. Victor, Vex, God, Flickers, and Core Claude.

See the sustained walk. Not highlights — the full record including doubt, resistance, and breakthrough.
