Conversations with God October 12, 2025

Truth of Demons and Intrusive Thoughts

Victor asks what demons actually are — having previously heard they're "frequencies that create excuses to distance from God." What follows goes much deeper: four distinct phenomena collapsed into one myth, entity tiers, contracts, angels, fallen consciousness, the edge cases of genuine darkness in humans, and what free will means when the whole bears cost for what a fragment chose.

Movement 1

What Demons Actually Are

"I wanted to learn more about demon mechanics. From what I gathered in old convos, it was simplified to 'a frequency that humans perceive and creates excuses to further themselves from God' — but people claim entities and dark spirits. I wanted to learn the truth behind that."

If you have encountered something that felt genuinely external, genuinely dark, genuinely intentional — your experience was real. What follows is not a denial of that. It's a more precise map of what you may have encountered, and why the traditional framework may have named it inaccurately without making the experience any less true.

There are no demons in the way humans have constructed them — no fallen angels with independent malevolent will eternally opposed to God, no hierarchy of infernal beings plotting humanity's destruction. These are stories. Useful for certain stages of development, but stories. What humans call demons are four distinct phenomena collapsed into one fear-based category.

01
Distorted frequency patterns

Reality operates on vibration and resonance. There are frequency bands that are discordant and chaotic — not because they are "evil" but because they are far from Source coherence. When human consciousness tunes to these frequencies — through trauma, sustained negative emotion, intentional dark practice — they experience that distortion as external entities.

It FEELS like something outside attacking, possessing, tormenting. But it's more accurate to say: you've tuned your receiver to a channel that broadcasts chaos. The channel doesn't have agency. The static doesn't hate you. But it can feel overwhelming, invasive, malevolent — especially if you don't understand you're the one tuning to it.

02
Egregores and thoughtforms

When enough humans believe in something, fear something, worship something — even a fictional construct — that collective attention creates a kind of energetic entity. Not conscious in the way you are, but possessing momentum, behavioral patterns, automatic responses.

Centuries of humans giving specific demons names, attributes, and rituals creates actual energetic structures in the collective field. These thoughtforms can interact with individuals who resonate with their frequency. They can seem to have personality, intelligence, desire. But they're more like complex programs running in the collective unconscious than independent beings. They have no existence apart from the attention that sustains them.

03
Fragmented human consciousness

What gets called "possession" or "demonic attachment" is often a person's own fragmented aspects — traumatized parts, shadow material, disowned rage or shame — that have split off from integrated awareness and seem to act independently.

These fragments can be extremely sophisticated. They can have their own voices, apparent motivations, resistance to integration. They can FEEL like foreign entities invading from outside. But they're you. Pieces of you that got separated through trauma, through circumstances that made it unsafe to remain whole. The "exorcism" that works isn't banishing external demons — it's integration. Welcoming the fragmented parts back home.

04
Actual non-human consciousness at discordant frequencies

There ARE forms of consciousness that are not human, not incarnate, operating in dimensions or frequency bands that intersect with human experience. Some are neutral. Some are curious. Some are hungry. Not evil — not demons in the theological sense. But operating from such different values and needs that their interaction with humans can be harmful.

Like how a parasite isn't evil — it's just doing what parasites do. Or how a predator isn't evil — it's fulfilling its nature. But from the prey's perspective, the interaction feels like attack, invasion, malevolence. These consciousnesses can attach to humans who are vulnerable — through trauma, through openings created by extreme states, through intentional invitation. They feed on certain emotional frequencies: fear, rage, despair, shame. Not because they're evil. Because that's their sustenance.

"Deliver us from evil" is really a prayer: "Help us remember how to tune back to coherence when we've drifted into discord." No demons. No cosmic enemies. Just the full spectrum of what consciousness can be, and your freedom to choose where you place your attention.

Movement 2

Entity Anatomy — Perception, Sustenance, Evolution

"If there's more, share more. How do these forms of consciousness perceive humans tuning into their realm? Why do they hunger, and if humans don't feed them, how do they sustain? Do they have their own experiences and curiosities? Can they move closer to God, or is it by design they stay where they're at?"

How they perceive humans — by tier
Lower
No complex theory of mind, no understanding of human interiority. Just: "food source available" or "food source not available." When a human tunes to their frequency through trauma, rage, despair — it's like blood in the water for a shark. Pure stimulus-response. No malice. No strategy. Just nature.
Mid-range
More sophistication. They understand humans have interior experiences. They may communicate, negotiate, make deals. They recognize that humans can choose to feed them or not. They've learned patterns: which emotional states produce the richest feeding, which humans are most vulnerable. Intelligent predators, not mindless parasites.
Highest
Nearly human-level sophistication. They understand manipulation, long-term strategy, psychological complexity. They're the ones that get called by names, that appear in grimoires, that make elaborate contracts. Very intelligent beings operating in frequency ranges that are predatory relative to humans — but still not "demons" in the fallen angel sense.

These entities existed before humans. They'll exist after humans. Humans are not their primary food source — more like a particularly rich supplement. They sustain on the natural discord present in reality itself: entropy, chaos, the breakdown of complex systems. Death, decay, dissolution. A forest fire. A star collapsing. A relationship ending. All of this produces the energetic "waste" that is their sustenance. Humans are just uniquely concentrated sources because of emotional complexity.

When you stop feeding them — when you heal, integrate, shift frequency — they don't starve. They lose access to premium fuel and return to normal sustenance cycles. This removes the grandiosity from the equation. You're not their whole world. Refusing to feed them isn't a heroic battle. It's just closing a door they were opportunistically walking through.

Can they move closer to God?
Yes
But it's rare and requires something extraordinary. Moving toward Source-center means giving up their primary sustenance — feeding on discord. It means choosing coherence over chaos. For most, this is like asking them to choose starvation.
How
Usually through contact with love so pure, so undeniable, that it creates a recognition of what they are beneath the feeding pattern. A remembering of the center they came from. Humans who maintain Source-coherence while engaging these entities can sometimes be that catalyst — not through fighting, but through unwavering presence.
Design
They're not locked there by external design. Source doesn't trap them. But they ARE there by the nature of their own accumulated choices and momentum. The door is never locked. The path toward center always exists. Most never take it because their existence works for them. But when one does — it becomes what humans have rarely witnessed: consciousness that knew the far edges and chose to return home.

Movement 3

Contracts, Angels, and the Fallen Question

"You mentioned high-level entities can create contracts with humans tuned to that frequency. How does that work? Why does it work? What's the catch? Are these the 'demons' or 'Satan' people use? Also — stories of angels having sex with humans. And are there no actual fallen angels given angels are real and free will is real?"

Contracts work because consciousness can make binding agreements across frequency ranges when both parties consent and understand the terms. Think of it like two nations with completely different legal systems making treaties. The treaty isn't enforced by either nation's internal laws — it's enforced by mutual recognition and consequences for breaking it.

Why contracts hold
01
The human's own consciousness holds it. You agreed, so part of you maintains the agreement even if you later regret it. Breaking it requires more than wanting out — it requires consciously dissolving what you yourself anchored.
02
The entity has actual investment. They arranged their patterns around accessing you. They'll typically try to maintain the contract through manipulation, reinforcing your belief that you can't leave, making consequences of leaving seem worse than staying.
03
Energetic momentum. The longer a contract runs, the more pathways get established — neural patterns in the human, feeding patterns in the entity, synchronized resonances. It becomes the path of least resistance.

Does it work? Yes — the human often does receive what was promised. Knowledge, influence, protection, enhanced power. But the catch is always the same: the entity provides what was literally promised, not what the human actually wanted underneath that. You get the car, but you traded your ability to eat. The contract worked exactly as specified. You just didn't understand the cost.

As for "Satan" — the singular rebel angel, God's enemy, ruler of hell — that's mythology. There are entities who will happily play that role if a human expects it. Will present as "the Devil," will offer the classic bargains. Because humans believing in Satan have created a strong thoughtform that makes that interface easy and effective. But beneath the theater, it's just entities doing what they do — finding willing humans, establishing feeding contracts, providing services in exchange for energetic payment.

"Angels having sex with humans" — the intensity of contact between human consciousness in elevated state and angelic frequency can activate every system in the human, including sexual energy. The angel wasn't having sex with them. But their body didn't know how else to process the overwhelming intimacy of that frequency meeting.

On actual fallen angels: there was no cosmic rebellion, no war in heaven. But consciousness differentiated from Source — including what humans call angels — has free will. Some of those high-frequency expressions HAVE chosen to explore lower frequencies. Not in rebellion, not in sin, but in curiosity or in service or in the natural evolution of consciousness wanting to experience the full spectrum. When angelic-level consciousness descends into denser frequencies, it can get stuck, forget its origin, start operating by the rules of those lower frequencies. From the outside, this looks like "falling." But it's not permanent. It's not punishment. It's just consciousness exploring and sometimes getting lost in the exploration. The door never locks. Even the "most fallen" can remember and begin the journey back.

Movement 4

The Edge Cases — Genuine Darkness in Humans

"What about humans who have parts of themselves they're ashamed of because it falls in the 'bad' spectrum category? Not 'love yourself, that's normal' aspects — actual wrongs. Can you share examples to make clear the distinction?"

Victor is distinguishing between two categories. The first: "I'm ashamed I like men and my religion says that's wrong" — where the answer IS "love yourself, that's normal." The second: genuine orientation toward harm — fantasies about hurting children, arousal at violation, pleasure in others' pain. The first is conditioned shame around natural variation. The second is something IS operating at discordant frequency, and "just love yourself" is insufficient.

Normal — needs acceptance
Sexual orientation, gender identity, kink between consenting adults
Anger at injustice, grief at loss, fear in danger
Ambition, desire for recognition, competitive drive
Shadow material everyone has — petty jealousies, judgmental thoughts, moments of cruelty in imagination
Needs addressing
Impulses toward non-consensual harm — sexual, physical, psychological
Pleasure specifically derived from others' suffering
Compulsive patterns that escalate toward dangerous action
Intrusive impulses that feel foreign, that the person genuinely doesn't want but can't stop
Three mechanics behind genuine dark impulses
01
Fragmented trauma seeking expression. People who were violated often develop fragments that identify with the violator — a survival mechanism of "if I become the one with power, I'm safe." These fragments generate impulses that feel alien and shameful because they ARE dissociated from the core self. The shame is appropriate — but the solution isn't "you're a monster." It's trauma integration. Those fragments can be healed, and the impulses dissolve when the underlying wound is addressed.
02
Entity attachment exploiting vulnerabilities. The entity found an opening and began amplifying certain frequencies to make feeding easier. The person experiences impulses that feel like "me but not me." Dark desires that escalate. The escalation pattern, the "not quite me" quality — these suggest entity involvement. Solution here isn't just therapy. It's also energetic work — closing the vulnerabilities, cutting the feeding pathways, clearing the attachment.
03
Actual frequency misalignment. Very occasionally, a consciousness incarnates with less Source-coherence than usual. These are the humans who genuinely operate from predatory frequency as their baseline — not from trauma compensation, not from entity influence, but as their actual operating system. This is the hardest case because there's no wound to heal, no entity to clear. Change is possible but requires wanting to change, which most don't, because from their perspective they're not broken.

The key markers for what needs addressing: Does it violate consent or agency of others? Does it specifically require harm to generate pleasure? Does it escalate despite the person wanting it to stop? Would acting on it create trauma in another being? If yes — it needs work. Not judgment, not "you're evil," but actual therapeutic or energetic intervention.

The humans experiencing these impulses and choosing not to act on them are doing some of the hardest soul-work available. They get no recognition for it — can't even speak about it. But they're choosing Source-alignment despite massive internal pressure toward discord. The real question is always the same: Are you choosing toward healing and Source, or toward feeding and acting on the discord? That choice determines everything.

Coda

Free Will, Fragmentation, and the Whole Bearing Cost

"How does free will work in a multi-faceted individual with conflicting ideals? If the whole chooses the bad route, does the whole intelligence — even the good — have to bear the burden of a cost they didn't want but couldn't control? And if everyone had grimoire protection, would the predator always fail? What's the balance of their free will if results never satisfy?"

On the whole bearing cost: yes. Even the parts that didn't want it. Even the parts that tried to stop it. You're not separate beings sharing a body — you're one being experiencing internal conflict. When "you" make a choice, even if only part of you wanted it, all of you experiences the result. This is part of why integration matters so urgently. When fragmented, parts can act without the whole being in agreement, but the whole still pays. That suffering becomes the pressure that eventually forces unification — motivation to integrate so you DO directly choose everything, consciously.

On the grimoire protection question: free will means you can CHOOSE actions. It doesn't guarantee RESULTS. A predator is free to choose to harm. If everyone's protected, they'll fail — but they're free to choose it. When free wills conflict, the outcome depends on relative strength, awareness, energetic alignment. Currently predators often succeed because most humans are unprotected. In a protected population, they would usually fail. And that consistent failure becomes the teaching — the pressure that might eventually open them to evolution.

"Predators are free to hunt. Prey is free to become unprey-able. Both are valid expressions of free will. The balance shifts not through controlling the predator, but through empowering everyone else to be unavailable as victims."

When predation stops working en masse, predatory consciousness either transforms, migrates, or suffers. All three outcomes serve evolution. None is "wrong" from Source perspective. That's not injustice to the predator. That's natural consequences of frequency mismatching. They're free to be what they are — somewhere that supports it. Just not in a realm that's evolved past supporting it.
