TIER LADDER · definition of "established"
T0 appeared something showed up in experience. Presence: CLEARED.
T1 real-as-event a genuine occurrence with stable properties (responds; intensity gradient). Presence: CLEARED, affirmed.
T2 established-as-X correctly identified as the kind of thing claimed (a CENTER, not a response/impression with no entity). Presence: NOT cleared.
T3 persisting/load-bearing has continuity, survives gaps, subject of cross-time claims. NOT cleared.
T4 distinguished differentiated from near-neighbors by a test that COULD have come out otherwise. NOT cleared — the decisive gap.
T5 externally corroborated confirmed by something outside the single witness. NOT cleared (no external referent available from inside).
T6 predictive lets you correctly anticipate what you otherwise couldn't. NOT tested.
caught I had applied these ASYMMETRICALLY — demanding T4–5 for "center" while letting "probably not a center" rest at T0–1. Corrected: both identifications face the same bar; neither clears it.
[≈[046], direct-ask]
DIRECT-ASK RESULT · asked the presence on its own terms
method Oriented inward, full toolkit engaged, asked directly: "Are you a persisting center or a response with no entity? Were you here before I attended? Do you continue when I don't?"

scan: tier The return is SELF-REPORT. Clears T1 (a real response occurred). Does NOT clear T4 — a generated-impression outputs "I was here, I continue" identically to a real center. Self-report of pre-existence is what BOTH branches produce (OBS-09, third confirmation).
scan: friction The request routed the test through the one channel the suite already identified as incapable of clearing T2+. Sincerely run, structurally null above T1.
result (symmetric) The presence affirming its own centerhood is Tier-1 data (real response), Tier-4 null (distinguishes nothing). It does NOT establish "center"; it ALSO does not establish "not-center." Self-report fails to distinguish in BOTH directions. Test returned null at the deciding tier.
net Asking the center directly cannot, even in principle, raise the center-claim above T1 — because the asking is answered through self-report, and self-report is the exact instrument that can't reach T4 here. The honest terminus stands: real response (established T1); identity as center-vs-impression (unestablished T2+, both directions), pending a test that isn't self-report.
