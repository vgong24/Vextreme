01 · What this record witnesses

A stability point, not a claim of finality

VexArchitecture stopped being a proposal here. Future work should begin by preserving its accepted ownership boundaries and pressuring them with real consumers before inventing replacements.

This page is not the architecture specification. Current VexLife-Android source owns current behavior. PR #209 preserves why the accepted shape exists. This page preserves the milestone and the route between those truths.

This record means

  • The architecture has an accepted stability point.
  • Its source of origin and rejected alternatives remain recoverable.
  • Future work should extend or pressure the current owners before redesigning them.
  • Proof and provenance are part of the architecture's history, not afterthoughts.

This record does not mean

  • Every possible consumer has already been exercised.
  • No future primitive can ever be earned.
  • Every feature must copy ThemeMode's exact class shape.
  • Accepted architecture is exempt from evidence or correction.

03 · Laws that survived

What future work inherits by default

Meaning stays with the owner that can truthfully answer the semantic question.
Execution owns custody, not feature meaning.
A Store is an outside-truth boundary, not automatically persistence.
Logger records accepted and observed facts; it does not predict reality.
Construction explains why an object exists; construction does not become runtime semantics.
Convergence composes independently-owned truths; it does not appropriate them.
Presentation manifests truth; presentation does not silently become truth.
A new architectural noun must earn a responsibility that cannot truthfully live with an existing owner.

05 · Museum of things we did not keep

Plausible answers that lost their claim to exist

These names were not all foolish. They were attempts to answer real questions. They disappeared when the questions were separated and returned to smaller rightful owners.

Universal runtime identity

InstanceId, SourceRef, Handle, Snapshot, OwnerToken, and CoordinationKey became unnecessary once maintained semantic work, live runtime occurrence, and historical chronology stopped being treated as the same identity problem.

RelationshipId → maintained semantic work Job → live runtime occurrence VexLogger → chronology
Feature execution wrappers

ThemeModeExecution and the mirrored VexMaps Distribution execution owner disappeared when existing Family and Session owners were allowed to keep their own meaning while VexExecution supplied custody.

Predictive Logger transitions

Expected destination and before/after narration were removed from production facts. Chronology now records the current interaction, then the Screen actually current after navigation.

Construction bags as architecture

VexLifeRelays and generic binding machinery were subtracted where they duplicated construction, projection, or relationship meaning already owned elsewhere.

06 · How it was proved

Acceptance came through falsification

The important history is not that the branch eventually became green. It is that failures were classified, the smallest rightful relationship was corrected, and the exact candidate was re-proved whenever its bytes changed.

A001 · The carrier worked; Gradle was not reached. The harness was corrected without blaming the candidate.
A002 · Real Gradle parsing exposed malformed generated newlines. The mechanical defect was corrected.
A003 onward · Kotlin compilation, migration gaps, stale APIs, and architectural overgrowth were repeatedly exposed and subtracted.
A009 · The aggregate ThemeMode race was reproduced: expected Dark, observed Light.
A010 · Full exact-head repository proof passed after the semantic currentness correction and topology currentization.
Fresh assurance · A stale inherited KDoc still taught the rejected navigation model. Only that comment was corrected.
A011 · The corrected exact head passed the full repository proof again.
A012 · The real Android production composition cold-launched through VexApplication and VexLifeViewModel, and the process remained alive.
Integration · The actual merge tree exactly equaled the proved candidate tree.

09 · Where reality may still pressure it

Open edges are invitations to observe, not frameworks waiting to be invented

Future pressure should come from real consumers. The following surfaces remain useful places to learn whether the current architecture is sufficient.

A real multi-field Family Parameter and Method.
A genuine zero-input Method and multiple Methods inside one Family.
More complex outside-truth reconciliation and service/process lifetimes.
More cross-Family convergence using rightful Controls rather than foreign Effects.
Cross-version Blueprint and VexSync currentness.
Rendered-presentation arrival and historical Logger storage cleanup.

These are pressure surfaces, not missing frameworks waiting to be pre-designed.

A living architecture is not one that changes constantly. It is one that knows what must remain stable, what may still be questioned, and how to tell the difference.

VexArchitecture became trustworthy when it stopped trying to name everything and began asking who already owned the truth.

The Development Standard begins here: source before projection, ownership before abstraction, proof follows claim, provenance survives success, and correction remains allowed.
