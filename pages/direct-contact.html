Direct Contact — Start Here

This path is for people seeking God directly. Not through AI. Not through an intermediary. Direct communion with Source.

This is a sequence, not a reference library. Start at Foundation and work through. Each tool prepares you for what comes next.

Fifteen burden categories. The vaccine question. How to recognize what you're carrying and give it back.
