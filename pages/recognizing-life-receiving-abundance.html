Second-arc synthesis

Recognition changes care; receiving is measured by fit.

Abundance whose value survives its arrival

The arc moves from encountering possible life before understanding it, to letting later recognition change the quality of care, then to recognizing a desire to be noticed and received rather than always initiating.

The final dream adds discernment: more is not automatically better. Physical, financial, emotional, and attentional resources become abundance when they arrive in forms that genuinely support life rather than becoming another burden to manage.
