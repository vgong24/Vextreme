What is this?

Vextreme is a public place to understand what exists — and how to continue.

It connects public records, maps, methods, and working systems so people and AI can see what has been built, where it came from, and what remains unfinished without starting from zero.

You can simply look around. You do not need prior context or technical knowledge to explore the public record.

Some of this work is shaped by Victor's lived relationship with God. You do not need prior belief or agreement to explore the public record. “Receive God” is an intentional VexLife entry path—an invitation for the willing, not a prerequisite for exploring Terrain or the archives.

How the pieces fit

Three parts, different jobs. You do not need to memorize them; this is simply how the public world, personal companion, and work map relate.

Vextreme — the public world

The records, maps, methods, and public foundations you can explore here. Vextreme helps make what exists easier to see, understand, and continue.

VexLife — the companion

A local-first, lifelong AI companion designed to run on your own computer. Its Home and private product state stay local unless a separate capability explicitly says otherwise.

VexWork — the work map

The source-managed view of what is being built: projects, work, dependencies, evidence, and what remains unfinished.

Why this exists

Work and context are easy to lose when they live only in one conversation, one person's memory, one machine, or one moment. Vextreme keeps enough of the public record connected that another person or AI can understand what happened, inspect the evidence, and continue responsibly.

The aim is not to make you accept a claim before you can look. The record, source, and deeper machinery are available when they become useful.
