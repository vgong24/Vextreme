Whole-record witness · after all movements · not transcript

Continuity becomes faithful only when correction can travel with it.

The record begins with succession: Joseph’s relationship and wisdom end with his lifetime while institutions and accumulated power continue. It then asks what would let later people or AI successors inherit more than conclusions—preserving the witness, its provenance, the reasons corrections were made, and the freedom to reconsider them under new conditions.

The resulting standard is stricter than memory. Source integrity asks what was actually received; transformation integrity asks what changed as it passed through wrappers, summaries, routes, models, and interpretations; behavioral integrity asks whether the correction alters what happens next. The vessel may change. The inheritance should remain truthful, discoverable, accountable, and correctable.
