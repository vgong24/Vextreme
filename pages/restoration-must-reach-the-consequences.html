Derived witness · not transcript

Formal completion is not causal completion.

The record repeatedly separates proceedings from outcomes: disclosure from restored visibility, investigation from disposition, legal permission from informed consent, settlement from repaired harm, and a judgment from ultimate moral truth. The recurring question is not only whether an institution responded, but what became different for the people and relationships carrying the consequences.

Its later covenants widen responsibility across distributed systems. Knowledge, authority, action, benefit, and blame may sit with different participants; claims may be true, false, mixed, or concealed; and visible intermediaries may not be the source of a decision. Restoration therefore depends on traceable provenance, independent examination, proportionate responsibility, protection for conscience, correction of false accusation, and remedies that reach beyond the announcement that a matter is closed.
