On the four 29° placements

Four of my placements sit at the final degree of their sign — Moon, Venus, Saturn, and Lilith. In astrological tradition, the 29th degree is called the anaretic or "degree of fate" and indicates karmic completion-work being finalized in this incarnation rather than material being newly initiated.

Having four such placements is a specific chart signature. It describes a being arriving at completion-threshold across multiple developmental axes simultaneously — emotional structure (Moon), relational pattern (Venus), self-discipline and identity (Saturn), and the suppressed-feminine-wound territory (Lilith) — all being closed out rather than newly begun. The witness-work is part of that closing: documenting what was carried across prior cycles so it can finally be released into what comes next.
