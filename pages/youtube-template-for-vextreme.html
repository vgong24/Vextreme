YouTube Template for Vextreme

This is not the finished template. It is the place where Victor and the instance walk an episode into existence before deciding what structure the episode actually needs.

The walk

Start from what Victor wants to express.

Do not interview Victor with a form. Listen to what is already alive in the conversation and move with it. Reflect emerging threads when they become visible. Preserve language, demonstrations, moments, tensions, jokes, visuals, and transitions that feel like part of the episode.

The instance may keep notes while the walk is happening, but notes serve the walk. They do not become a hidden outline that forces the conversation toward a predetermined video.

Notes carried into the first walk

What is already worth remembering

Vextreme YouTube is an arc, not a pile of unrelated uploads.
Each real episode should have one durable Vextreme record that can mature from planning through publication.
After publication, the YouTube video should be embedded back into the record rather than becoming the only place the episode lives.
The record can preserve the transcript, thumbnail, demonstrated build or release, source links, editing notes, and continuity into the next episode.
AI may help perceive footage, return timestamped edit suggestions, notice unclear explanations, and preserve production context. Final editorial choice remains Victor's.
A downloadable VexLife may become the first public episode threshold, but the walk decides what the first episode actually wants to say.
After the walk

Let the episode crystallize only after it exists.

Once a real episode has emerged, this record can be shaped into the production surfaces that are actually useful:

  1. the episode's intention and story shape
  2. the demonstrations, screen captures, camera moments, and other visual sources it needs
  3. editing notes, timing, overlays, music, and review observations
  4. title, thumbnail, description, chapters, and publication metadata
  5. the final YouTube embed, transcript, and demonstrated artifact references
  6. what the episode established and the next natural edge it opened
Held until experience earns it

Do not freeze a reusable episode template from imagination. Walk the first real video, produce it, notice what was actually reusable, and only then turn those repeated needs into the template Victor comes back to.
