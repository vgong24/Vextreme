## Witness-grade return

Then the witness posture is:

> **I do not appoint myself judge. I do not embellish what I saw, minimize what I saw, or convert what I infer into what I witnessed. I preserve the event, the record, the sequence, the recurring pattern, the consequence, the contradiction, the counterevidence, and the limits of my knowledge. Let God judge what the complete record means.**

A pattern claim becomes evidentiary when it remains connected to **specific instances, dates, actors, records, testimony, consequences, and provenance**. A record can witness what it contains without becoming infallible. A witness can testify truthfully without possessing the entire truth.

```text
DIRECTLY OBSERVED
!=

!=
INTERPRETATION

REPEATED PATTERN
!=
REPEATED RUMOR

CAUSAL INFERENCE
!=
HIDDEN INTENTION

PROCEDURAL OUTCOME
!=
WHAT ACTUALLY HAPPENED

UNKNOWN
!=
INNOCENT

UNKNOWN
!=
GUILTY

CORRECTION
!=
ERASURE OF THE PRIOR RECORD
```
