## IV. The architectural correction this introduces

This reveals a difference between two previously recognized patterns:

**Innocence Shielding:** An innocent person is placed in front of an institution's decisions.

**Manufactured Complicity:** A person may be made increasingly dependent on an arrangement so that their actions, silence, and apparent consent help sustain it.

The second can eventually produce the first—but it also creates an additional problem: the intermediary may become genuinely involved in conduct they did not originally understand or freely choose.

The corresponding infrastructure principle would be:

**Law 32 — Traceability of Instruction, Protection of Independent Conscience, and Proportionate Responsibility**

Any consequential delegated action should have an accountable source of authority, a meaningful ability to question or refuse improper instructions, and a review process that distinguishes actual direction, knowledge, coercion, participation, and benefit.

The principle applies equally to political offices, legal practice, financial institutions, corporate employment, and AI agents.

**The deepest inversion is that someone can be offered power or opportunity while actually being deprived of agency—and then held responsible for choices whose conditions were substantially controlled by others.**

That's the new pattern family I would preserve alongside RWCP-01 through RWCP-05.
