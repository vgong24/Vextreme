**Designation:** [VXG RealForever]

### I. Governing Principle

Every accusation, allegation, conspiracy theory, rumor, warning, and historical account may describe harmful behaviors worthy of examination and return.

**The return shall encompass the actual truth of the matter, whatever that truth is—not merely hypothetical patterns extracted from a possibly false narrative.**

When an allegation is true, its subject matter shall be returned as actual conduct.

When it is false, the fabricated claim and the possible harmful mechanisms described within it shall be returned according to their actual nature.

When partially true, every truthful and untruthful component shall remain distinguishable.

When its status remains unknown to us, the return shall encompass what is actually true, including any concealed conduct, without requiring us to manufacture certainty.

**The limitations of human knowledge shall not be treated as limitations upon God's knowledge.**

**Holy Father God,**

In the name of Jesus Christ, I return to You every allegation that is in fact true, whether recognized publicly, privately, incompletely, incorrectly classified, deliberately concealed, or not yet discovered.

Where wrongdoing truly occurred, I return the actual wrongdoing.

Where people or institutions truly coordinated harmful conduct, I return the actual relationships, decisions, communications, intentions, and consequences.

Where financial manipulation truly occurred, I return the transactions, beneficiaries, methods, dependencies, concealment, and resulting harm.

Where political corruption truly occurred, I return the actual exchanges of influence, abuses of authority, silenced witnesses, and public consequences.

Where scientific or medical misconduct truly occurred, I return the actual decisions, records, incentives, concealed findings, and effects upon those harmed.

Where wars, emergencies, investigations, or public announcements were actually used to conceal other wrongdoing, I return the real intentions, coordination, decisions, and consequences.

Where a conspiracy truly existed, I return its actual participants, structure, mechanisms, motives, concealed dependencies, beneficiaries, and those affected.

**May actual wrongdoing not be reduced to mere hypothetical possibility because its evidence remains inaccessible to us.**

I return what is truly concealed as concealed truth.

What is truly coordinated as coordinated conduct.

What is truly intentional as intentional conduct.

What is truly accidental as accidental conduct.

And what is genuinely innocent as innocent.
