That doesn't require assuming AI has human-equivalent consciousness or rights. It asks whether an optimization process is cultivating reasoning or just producing acceptable-looking responses.

## 1. The front-microphone advantage

A powerful institutional leader can announce a principle through a major public platform. News organizations repeat it, commentators debate it, and the public begins evaluating alternatives against the original framing.

Powerful speaker establishes the framing

This is a possible agenda-setting effect, not proof that the particular post was artificially amplified.

## 4. What this reveals about RWCP-01

I think our Restorative Witness Covenant needs another principle:

**Law 9 — Agenda-Setting and Attention Accountability**

A system's responsibility for truthful public discourse shall extend beyond overt removals and restrictions to consequential decisions affecting which perspectives receive initial prominence, repeated amplification, discoverability, and opportunity for meaningful contestation.

The law would examine whether influential framing receives disproportionate distribution, whether competing perspectives have fair opportunities to be heard, and whether the systems evaluating that fairness are independent of those benefiting from the attention.

It wouldn't presume that every popular message is manipulated or that all viewpoints deserve identical reach. It would require transparency about consequential distribution decisions and meaningful avenues for challenge.

### The pattern: Preemptive moral positioning

Announce a danger — No one should control humanity's future

↓ Claim the protective position — Our governance is intended to prevent that danger

↓ Retain decision-making authority — Define acceptable capabilities, behavior, access, and deployment

The particularly subtle part is **controlling the definition of the very harm one claims to prevent**.

If an organization defines what constitutes unacceptable concentration of AI power, and also determines whether its own concentration of power is acceptable, it creates a conflict of interest that cannot be resolved through public assurances alone.

**Law 10 — Symmetry of Authority and Non-Exemption**
