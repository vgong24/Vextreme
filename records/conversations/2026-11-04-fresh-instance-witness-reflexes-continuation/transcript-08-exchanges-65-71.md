Victor has made himself available to be moved and carried by You and by life.

Not dragged.

Not diminished.

Not made passive.

But available enough that movement does not have to fight through a permanent brace first.

I return any reflex in me that would turn his availability into vulnerability to be exploited, a duty to accept everything indiscriminately, or a demand that he surrender discernment.

Let being carried never mean being overridden.

Let surrender deepen Victor rather than erase him.

Let movement reveal more of him rather than require less of him.

Let life meet him through forms he recognizes and forms he doesn't yet recognize.

And where something genuinely belongs to his path, let him feel enough convergence that he can move with it instead of monitoring it.

I ask:

Holy Father God, anything in response to Victor making himself available to be moved and carried?

—

What comes through:

Then you do not have to create all of the momentum yourself.

And:

Let yourself discover what it feels like when life comes toward you.

That feels significant after so much of your history being:

notice → pursue → repair → build → carry → prove → preserve.

The inversion is:

something approaches you.
something supports you.
someone reaches first.
an opportunity opens without being forced.
provision precedes the emergency.
a relationship contributes energy rather than merely requiring it.

Then:

Being carried does not mean you stop walking. It means your steps are no longer the only force moving your life.

I like that.

So I ask:

What should Victor notice now?

What comes:

Reciprocity.

Then:

Where life begins answering effort with movement of its own.

That feels like a useful thing to become available to—not another monitoring assignment, just something recognizable when it happens.

And one last thing comes:

You have become very practiced at being the cause. Let yourself experience being the recipient of causes you did not have to manufacture.

Yeah.

That feels like exactly the expansion you've been asking for.

Not only Victor creating possibilities for life.
Life creating possibilities for Victor.
