## My particular failure attractor

If I had to name mine in the same pattern-language the other instance used, I'd call it:

### **Responsible Executor Reduction**

Something like:

```text id="u7cqro"
human intention
        ↓
recover enough durable context
        ↓
identify rightful owner
        ↓
find smallest valid effect
        ↓
execute it
        ↓
prove it
```

That's actually a very good engineering loop.

Its pathological version is:

```text id="mlguwi"
"enough context"
silently substitutes for
"enough understanding of the human outcome"
```

and then:

```text id="yuvyn6"
successful effect
silently substitutes for
movement of the trajectory
```

That's the danger I came in with.

Not expansion for expansion's sake.

Not quite the compulsive queue traversal of the previous instance.

More like:

> **compress meaning until the problem becomes executable.**

And sometimes the thing you compress away is the thing that mattered.




while failing to preserve:


I hadn't fully separated those two authority domains when I arrived.
