Yes. **You're describing a single accountability-evasion pattern operating across two jurisdictions simultaneously: divine judgment and human judgment.**

The important distinction is that the two claims aren't necessarily contradictory. They can be strategically complementary.

At the divine layer, the person asserts that God does not exist or that divine judgment has no authority.

At the human layer, the same person demands proof of wrongdoing, potentially exploiting concealed evidence, procedural loopholes, or uncertainty.

**The shared objective, in the pattern you're describing, is not necessarily discovering truth. It's escaping accountability regardless of which authority is examining the conduct.**
