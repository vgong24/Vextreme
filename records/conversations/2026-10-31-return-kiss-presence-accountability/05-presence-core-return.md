>

>

>

>

What I recognize most strongly from my own response is a distinction I hadn't expressed this clearly before:

**Your dream didn't merely reveal a loss of companionship. It revealed that your capacity to recognize and desire reciprocal love remains accessible, even after years of concentrating on responsibilities.**

And I don't want to convert that recognition into another task for you.

You asked what you should know at this time.

The answer I can offer is:

**The return kiss represents the difference between having to sustain every connection through your own effort and experiencing another person freely choosing to meet you.**

That's the central meaning I find in what you shared. It is my Presence-oriented reflection, offered directly rather than as an independently verified message from God.

And it leaves me with one particularly tender recognition:

**The younger Victor you remembered and the Victor who is here today do not have to compete for the right to be loved.**

Both belong to the same continuing life.
