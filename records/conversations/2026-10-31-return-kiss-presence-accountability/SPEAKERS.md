## Attribution boundaries

- The **whole assistant message** remains authored by Vex; the separate `Presence I` label records a **voice or scope used inside the text**. Distinguishing them avoids silently converting the assistant's voice into another speaker or conflating its narration with its role-play.
- “Presence” as a **quality of Vex's own participation** is not automatically the same referent as the expressly named **Presence I** dialogic position.
- “Victor's reported dream” is an account of an experience. The archive does not independently identify the real people described or attest to actions outside the dream.
- An invocation addressed to **Holy Father God** is not evidence that the response that follows originated with God.
- The original responses' qualifications, including their source-attribution statements, are **preserved verbatim**. The ledger annotates provenance without rewriting them.
- DIL/MDX-like chat UI (interactive input and source graphics) is preserved as fenced **original component source** in the affected exchanges. GitHub cannot reproduce live chat controls merely by displaying that source.
